## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS with a small amount of vanilla JS, no build step. Hosted on Netlify by manual folder upload (drag and drop). The new site lives in `portfolio-v2/` and goes to a separate Netlify project; the original site (`site/`) stays live at adeyemiadebanjo-portfolio.netlify.app.

## Users

UK recruiters, hiring managers and interview panels for project delivery, operations and maintenance, construction and IT-enabled change roles in utilities and infrastructure. They usually arrive from a CV or LinkedIn link, on a desktop between other candidates, or on a phone, and decide within a minute whether the experience is real and relevant.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Adeyemi Sakiru Adebanjo that shows evidence of delivery across three areas: electricity distribution networks (O&M, incident response, capital works), construction, and IT/systems project management. Success is a recruiter contacting him.

## Positioning

An engineer-turned-project-manager who has held a live distribution network, built civil and electrical works on it, and studied project management formally (MSc, PRINCE2, Agile). Field photographs are his own.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Single page, sections: networks, construction and IT project management, career programme, field photographs, about, contact.
- Must work at 320px width and with keyboard and screen readers.
- No CV file is published; the CV is "available on request".

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Adeyemi Sakiru Adebanjo ("Ade Adebanjo" as alternate). Based in Sheffield, UK.

## Product Principles

1. Evidence over adjectives: every claim ties to a role, date, project or photo.
2. Three disciplines, one person: networks, construction and IT read as one career, not three CVs.
3. Honest gaps: illustrative graphics are labelled as illustrative.
