About

Engineer by training, project manager by trade. I started on consulting projects in Lagos and learned early that a project is decided long before anyone turns a spanner.

Five years of project administration at Lahor Limited taught me the control side of delivery: the registers, the minutes, the status reports. A project you cannot evidence is a project you cannot defend.

The years that shaped me were the eleven at Ibadan Electricity Distribution Company. A distribution network is not a project that finishes; it is an asset you keep alive while you rebuild parts of it. It also taught me that the technical problem is rarely the hard one. The hard one is the sponsor, the regulator, the contractor, the crew and the customer all needing a different account of the same outage, at the same time.

What I care about is the unglamorous part. Assurance checkpoints before handover, not defect lists afterwards. A RAID log that somebody actually reads. An incident report that changes the maintenance plan instead of filling a folder.

I moved to Sheffield for an MSc in Project Management at Sheffield Hallam, completed in 2026, and I am working toward project delivery, construction and IT-enabled change roles in UK utilities and infrastructure.
